Pangenome projects

The inverse of this page's headline finding. Most "new" genomes turn out to be re-hosted copies of something already in NCBI — pangenome projects are the opposite: registered at NCBI as BioProjects, but with the assemblies deposited elsewhere, so an NCBI-only count misses them entirely. Matched on the projects' own title/description text (pan-?genome), not a curated accession list, so new deposits appear automatically.

20 of 21 pangenome BioProjects registered at NCBI have zero SRA runs and zero assemblies in this pipeline — the records exist, the data does not follow. Publication coverage

Which NCBI BioProjects have a known paper, found local-first (CannabisGDB citations, GigaDB BioProject cross-refs), then NCBI's elink (dbfrom=bioproject → db=pubmed, computed links), then a PMC full-text search for the accession string itself -- one row per paper (a BioProject can have more than one), newest first; undated papers (GigaDB citations carry no year) sort last. The PMC step matters: elink's computed links have real, confirmed gaps -- e.g. Cannbio-2's BioProject (PRJNA667278) is explicitly named in its own paper's Data Availability text, yet elink finds nothing in *either* direction (bioproject→pubmed or pubmed→bioproject). PubMed itself only indexes title/abstract (a plain search for the accession finds nothing there), but PMC indexes full text -- searching it directly for each BioProject accession recovered 71 additional BioProjects that neither local signals nor elink had caught, taking coverage from 18% to 43%. Still a lower bound, not a true rate -- a BioProject with no hit here may still have a paper that isn't open-access in PMC, or doesn't use PMC's indexed accession format. Deliberately does NOT use SRA runinfo's own Study_Pubmed_id column -- verified it's corrupted (its two values resolve to unrelated 1975 biochemistry papers). Where a BioProject has more than one paper, deposit flags the one most likely to describe the original deposit (closest to when the data first appeared) vs. reuse for later papers that just used already-public data -- a heuristic, not a fact. "First appeared" deliberately isn't just BioProject registration_date: that field can reflect a later re-curation rather than the true original submission (PRJNA73819's reads 2018, seven years after van Bakel et al.'s actual 2011 genome project), so this takes whichever is earlier, that date or the earliest linked SRA run's release date.

121/282 BioProjects (43%) have at least one known paper -- but that covers 69% of all SRA runs, since the BioProjects found this way tend to be the larger, more actively-reused ones. The rest (161) are data-only as far as we can tell -- not necessarily unpublished, just not traceably linked -- but every one of them still has a recorded owner in the table above, so provenance isn't lost even without a citation.

Data beyond raw reads: EVA, TSA, GEO, ArrayExpress & oneKP

Five data types this pipeline had no visibility into before: variant/genotype calls at EBI's European Variation Archive (EVA), assembled transcripts in NCBI's TSA (Transcriptome Shotgun Assembly) database, processed expression/epigenomic result files (count matrices, peak calls, etc.) in NCBI's GEO, old microarray studies in EBI BioStudies/ArrayExpress, and de-novo-assembled transcriptomes from the 1000 Plants (oneKP) initiative hosted on CNGB's FTP. only data source marks a BioProject with zero SRA runs against it -- this row is the *only* data our pipeline has for it, not an extra layer on top of reads already tracked. new BioProject marks one not in bioprojects.csv at all: GEO SuperSeries GSE249738's BioProject (PRJNA1050262) has no organism tag of its own -- it's an umbrella project bundling a multi-species ChIP-seq methods paper (Arabidopsis, tomato, hemp) -- so our BioProject-level Cannabis[Organism] search structurally can't find it; only 6 of its 229 samples are actually hemp (counted from sample titles, not assumed). GEO's n_samples column reads "cannabis/total" for any series spanning more than one species. BioStudies' organism-tagged Cannabis hits are mostly its own mirrors of GEO series already counted above (2 of 3, filtered out of the table below) -- the one shown is a 2007 cDNA microarray study that predates SRA/GEO entirely, so there's no raw-read equivalent to find anywhere else. oneKP's 4 samples' raw reads are already known (BioProject PRJEB21674, already in bioprojects.csv) -- only their assembled transcriptomes are new.

4 distinct BioProjects (PRJEB49958, PRJEB78836, PRJEB90527, PRJNA1050262) have zero SRA runs against them -- EVA/TSA/GEO are the only data this pipeline has for them, including Phylos Bioscience's public genotype dataset (2,337 samples) -- 2,827 samples across all 2 EVA studies.
